# Up Next Flash

Up Next with a flash. Your next meeting in the menu bar, with a countdown and
one-click join, plus a red dot at the two moments meetings are actually
missed: as one comes up, and again as it starts. A click on the flashing item
mutes that flash; both moments are yours to set.

It finds the video link in the location, URL or notes (Zoom, Meet, Teams,
Webex and a dozen more), counts down to it, and can open the call for you
when it starts.

- **Needs:** your calendars in Calendar.app, through the host, which asks for
  access once, and permission to add events (for Add Calendar Event). No
  network.
- **Surfaces:** menu bar, panel, window, widget, settings.
- **The flash:** a red dot shows beside the countdown as a meeting comes up
  (5 minutes before by default), and again as the meeting starts, for its
  first two minutes. A left click while it shows mutes that one flash — the
  coming-up flash alone, so the meeting still flashes as it starts. Muting is
  per meeting: the next one always flashes. The dot is the one red thing the
  menu bar truly draws — colors and symbol effects are stripped there — and
  it can be turned off entirely.
- **Settings:** flash on or off, and each flash moment picked from 60, 45,
  30, 15, 10, 5, 1 or 0 minutes before; calendars to hide, all-day and
  declined events, only meetings with a link or with other people, the
  reminder lead time, auto-join, a full-screen alert, how the title shows in
  the menu bar, and opening calls in their apps.
- **Commands:** Join Next Meeting, Copy Next Meeting Link, Today's Meetings,
  Add Calendar Event ("Lunch with Sam tomorrow 1pm for 45m", "Offsite fri"
  for all day) and Snooze Meeting Reminder. No default hotkey, so it can sit
  beside Up Next; give one to any command in Stackbar's Settings, or run them
  from Spotlight, Shortcuts or a `stackbar://` link.
- **Agent tools:** `next_meeting`, `agenda` and `join_next`, off until you
  turn them on in Settings → Agents.
- **Requires:** Stackbar 0.27.0 or later.

## Highlights

### A flash you can't miss, and can mute
The menu bar item shows a red dot as a meeting comes up and again as it
starts. One click on the flashing item silences that flash — the start flash
still fires after a coming-up one is muted, and the next meeting starts fresh.
